Get your file from Instagram

  1. In Instagram, open Accounts Center, then Your information and permissions.
  2. Choose Export your information and pick your account.
  3. Select only Followers and following.
  4. Pick any date range, then set the format to JSON. All time gives the most complete results, because a shorter range may leave out people who followed before it started.
  5. When Meta says your file is ready, download the .zip. This can take a few minutes or a couple of days.

What happens to your file

  • You never sign in to Instagram here.
  • Your file stays on your device. This site has no upload feature and no server to send it to.
  • Only your followers and following lists are opened. Messages, photos and everything else in the file stay unread.
  • Nothing is saved. Closing this page clears your results.

Check it yourself: Once this page has loaded, turn on airplane mode and choose your file. It still works.
